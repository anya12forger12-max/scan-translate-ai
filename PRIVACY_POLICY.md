## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### A. Account Information
* **Registration Data:** Email address, display name, and a securely encrypted password representation managed by Firebase Authentication.
* **Security Credentials:** Industry-standard hashing secures your password. Plaintext passwords are never stored.
* **Session and Access Tokens:** Token identifiers maintain secure, authenticated sessions across your devices.

### B. Scan and Translation Data
* **Scanned Content:** QR codes, barcodes, and recognized text from camera-captured images.
* **Translation History:** Source text, translated output, and language pairs you use.
* **OCR Results:** Text extracted from images using on-device ML Kit processing.

### C. Device Settings and Preferences
* **App Preferences:** Theme selection, language preferences, and display options.

### D. Usage Analytics and Telemetry
* **Interaction Logs:** Features accessed, scan counts, translation counts, error logs, and loading durations.

## 2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

1. **Service Delivery:** Providing QR/barcode scanning, OCR text recognition, and real-time translation.
2. **History Management:** Storing your scan and translation history for quick access and re-use.
3. **Platform Optimization:** Improving OCR accuracy, translation quality, and app performance.

## 3. DEVICE PERMISSIONS REQUESTED

* **Camera Permission:** Essential for QR/barcode scanning and OCR text recognition. Camera access is requested at the point of use, not at install time.
* **Microphone Permission:** Required for voice translation features. If denied, voice translation is disabled but all other features remain available.
* **Notification Permission:** Used for scan completion alerts and app updates.

## 4. ADMINISTRATOR ACCESS AND DATA SECURITY

### A. Special Administrative Access
**You acknowledge and explicitly agree that authorized system administrators may access, view, or process stored user data solely for the purposes of support maintenance, legal compliance, abuse prevention, and platform security checks. Access is restricted, logged, and auditable.**

### B. Ownership of Content
You retain full ownership of your scanned content and translations. You grant Scan & Translate AI a limited license to process and display your content solely for delivering the Service.

### C. Data Protection and Security Controls
* **Communication Security:** All traffic is encrypted using TLS over HTTPS.
* **On-Device Processing:** OCR text recognition uses Google ML Kit, which processes images locally without sending them to external servers.
* **Firebase Storage:** Scan history is stored securely in Firebase with encrypted storage and strict per-user access controls.
* **Access Isolation:** Users can only access their own data. One user can never view another user's information.
